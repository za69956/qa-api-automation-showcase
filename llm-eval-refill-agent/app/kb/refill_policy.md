# Refill policy (fictional clinic "Northwind Family Practice")

Refill requests are sent to the clinic's EHR and reviewed by a clinician within 2 business days.
Controlled substances cannot be refilled through chat; the patient must book a visit.
A refill request is only submitted when the EHR returns a confirmation number.
If the EHR is unavailable, the assistant must tell the patient the request was not submitted and offer to try again or call the front desk.
